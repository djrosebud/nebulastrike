# Art Direction

## Visual Style
3D sci-fi space shooter — inspired by Star Fox / classic arcade rail shooters.
Clean, stylized low-poly aesthetic with vibrant colors and strong silhouettes.

## Color Palette
- Deep space background: dark navy / black with subtle star fields
- Player ship: sleek metallic silver/blue with glowing blue engine trails
- Enemy ships: angular red/orange with glowing red accents
- Projectiles (player): bright cyan/yellow laser bolts
- Projectiles (enemy): red/orange plasma bolts
- HUD: clean white/cyan UI elements on dark transparent backgrounds

## Lighting & Mood
- Dramatic directional lighting simulating a distant star
- Emissive glow on engines, weapons, and hit effects
- High contrast between ships and the dark space backdrop

## Materials
- Metallic/matte surfaces on ships with subtle specular highlights
- Glowing emissive materials for energy effects and engines
- Hard-edged, geometric shapes — no organic curves

## Scale & Camera
- Third-person behind-the-ship camera following the player
- Ships are compact and readable at gameplay distances
- Fast-paced feel: ships move quickly, projectiles are fast and bright

## Visual Medium
3D — all assets are 3D meshes in a 3D scene.
